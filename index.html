Talk to a real person — for free

This tool only shows what you might qualify for. To get actual help, these services are free and unbiased:

  • HUD-approved housing counselor (free): call the Homeowner's HOPE™ Hotline at 888-995-HOPE (4673), or find a counselor near you ↗.
  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: consumerfinance.gov ↗ — plain-language guides on your rights and options.
  • Your mortgage servicer: the number is on your monthly statement. Ask about "loss mitigation" or "home-retention options" — and ask for any offer in writing.
