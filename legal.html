Privacy Policy

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking, no advertising, and no forms that send data anywhere. Every calculation runs entirely in your web browser, on your device. Nothing you type into the tool — loan figures, dates, answers — ever leaves your device, and we never see it.

The one thing saved on your device

The site saves a single non-personal item in your browser's local storage: the current published mortgage interest-rate figure and the date it was fetched, so the tool loads faster. It contains no information about you. Clearing your browser data removes it. No consent banner is shown because there is nothing to consent to — this storage is purely functional and is never transmitted.

"Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control

We do not track visitors, so browser "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals have no effect on this site — there is nothing to opt out of.
